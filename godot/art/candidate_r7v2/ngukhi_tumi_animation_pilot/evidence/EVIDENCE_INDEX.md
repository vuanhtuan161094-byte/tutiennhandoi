## Limitations visible in the evidence

- The left arm is one shoulder-pivot cutout; no elbow, wrist or finger articulation is present.
- The hand remains open. The contact sheet does not prove a hand seal or a final production-quality Cast gesture.
- B is not an AI video. The right-side image is a static reference, and the AVI is generated by Godot from A.
- The full game verification gate passed its current candidate, but reports `Baseline status: NOT APPROVED` for Home. This pilot does not approve that baseline.
