## What this research can support

The best current transferable tests, not universal facts, are: (1) tell before hit; (2) distinguish player, Boss, projectile, impact and result lanes; (3) make challenge variation/rewards readable; (4) validate portrait silhouette and contrast in real runtime. No reference asset/code/UI was copied. A later pass should play actual footage for entries marked low before changing animation or audio direction.
