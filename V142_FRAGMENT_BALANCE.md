# V142 fragment balance rationale

Decision: keep **50 same-rank fragments per finished item**.

Why 50 works:

1. The number is readable and gives visible progress (1/50, 17/50, 49/50).
2. Realm fragments are separate by rank, so a Sáng Thế item still costs 50 Sáng Thế fragments instead of showing a quadrillion universal fragments.
3. The old `x3` rule is preserved through refinement: 3 lower-rank fragments -> 1 next-rank fragment.
4. Thus equivalent cost remains exact: Luyện Khí = 50 Luyện Khí = 150 Phàm; Sáng Thế = 50 Sáng Thế = 1,143,839,622,748,050 Phàm equivalent.
5. Hunt controls pacing through rarity, not through an unreadable craft-cost number.
6. Same-rank Hunt fragments are intentionally rare (about 0.6% of fragment events in zone 1 rising to 2.0% in zone 8), while lower-rank fragments remain the majority.

This lets a player occasionally hit a valuable current-realm fragment while still accumulating lower fragments for refinement, side equipment, and long-run progression.
